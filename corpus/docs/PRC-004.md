# Robot Hardware Leasing

Halcyon Robotics Pricing. Document PRC-004, effective 2024-04-01.

Leasing a Courier C1 costs 890 USD per month.

Leasing a Lift L1 costs 2,150 USD per month.

The minimum lease term is 24 months.

Related documents: PRD-001, PRD-009, PRC-007.

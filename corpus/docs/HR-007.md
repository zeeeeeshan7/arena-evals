# On-Call Compensation

Halcyon Robotics HR policy. Document HR-007, effective 2025-01-01.

Engineers are paid 150 USD for each weekend on-call shift.

Each on-call rotation has at least 6 engineers.

Related documents: ORG-003, INC-001.

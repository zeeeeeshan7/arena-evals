# Postmortem: Courier C2 Battery Shortfall

Halcyon Robotics Incident postmortem. Document INC-004, effective 2025-05-15.

Field tests measured an average Courier C2 runtime of 12.3 hours.

The shortfall was reported by 9 customers.

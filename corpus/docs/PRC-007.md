# Volume Discounts

Halcyon Robotics Pricing. Document PRC-007, effective 2024-04-01.

Orders of more than 100 robots receive a 10% discount on hardware.

Orders of more than 500 robots receive a 15% discount on hardware.

Related documents: PRC-004.

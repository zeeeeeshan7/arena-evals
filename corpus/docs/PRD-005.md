# Dock Charging Station

Halcyon Robotics Product specification. Document PRD-005, effective 2023-09-01.

The Dock charges any Halcyon robot from empty to full in 90 minutes.

One Dock serves up to 4 robots.

Related documents: PRD-001, PRD-004.

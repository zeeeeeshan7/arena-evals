# Courier C1 Delivery Robot

Halcyon Robotics Product specification. Document PRD-001, effective 2023-09-01.

The Courier C1 carries a maximum payload of 25 kg.

The Courier C1 has a top speed of 6 km/h.

A full charge runs the Courier C1 for 10 hours.

Related documents: PRD-005, PRC-004.

# Lift L1 Warehouse Robot

Halcyon Robotics Product specification. Document PRD-009, effective 2025-02-01.

The Lift L1 lifts pallets of up to 600 kg.

The Lift L1 reaches shelves up to 3.2 m high.

Related documents: PRD-005, PRC-004.
